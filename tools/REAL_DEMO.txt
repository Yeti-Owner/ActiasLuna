ALREADY DONE FOR YOU (laptop side):

  - the Uno Q white button toggles the simulated crowd

------------------------------------------------
  STEP 0 - ONE-TIME, DO WHILE YOU STILL HAVE INTERNET
------------------------------------------------
  Open PowerShell AS ADMINISTRATOR (right-click > Run as administrator) and run:

    New-NetFirewallRule -DisplayName "Moth boards" -Direction Inbound -Action Allow -Protocol UDP -LocalPort 5006,5007
    New-NetFirewallRule -DisplayName "Moth phone"  -Direction Inbound -Action Allow -Protocol TCP -LocalPort 8081

  (Lets the boards' data and your phone reach the server. Do this once.)

------------------------------------------------
  STEP 1 - POWER THE HARDWARE
------------------------------------------------
  - Power on ALL 3 ESP32 boards (A = AP makes the network, B and C = receivers).
  - Plug the Uno Q into the laptop by USB.

------------------------------------------------
  STEP 2 - PUT THE LAPTOP ON THE DEMO WI-FI
------------------------------------------------
  - Windows Wi-Fi > connect to  WATCHING-YOU  (disconnect EmoryGuest/eduroam first).
    Password = whatever Callum flashed into the boards.
  - Confirm you got a demo address:   ipconfig
      Wi-Fi IPv4 must be  192.168.4.x   (NOT 172.x / 169.254.x).
  - If it says 169.254.x, wait 20 s and reconnect. If 172.x, you're still on the
    other network - disconnect it.
  - WRITE DOWN your 192.168.4.x address - the phone needs it in Step 4.
  - (First time only) set this network to Private, in the Admin PowerShell:
      Get-NetConnectionProfile                 (find WATCHING-YOU's InterfaceIndex)
      Set-NetConnectionProfile -InterfaceIndex <that number> -NetworkCategory Private

------------------------------------------------
  STEP 3 - HOST THE SERVER
------------------------------------------------
    cd C:\Users\nicho\OneDrive\Documents\GitHub\ActiasLuna
    py tools/demo_run.py --button

  Open the dashboard on the laptop:   http://127.0.0.1:8080
  Within a few seconds the 3 board dots should turn GREEN (online). If they stay
  grey, see TROUBLESHOOTING in DEMO_STEPS.txt (usually not on 192.168.4.x, or firewall).

  Set the boards to their REAL spots: dashboard > Setup/Layout tab, drag each board
  (A/B/C) to where it physically sits in the room. Accuracy depends on this.

------------------------------------------------
  STEP 4 - JOIN THE PHONE AND CALIBRATE
------------------------------------------------
  - On the PHONE: connect to Wi-Fi  WATCHING-YOU.
  - Open the phone's browser to:   https://<your 192.168.4.x from Step 2>:8081
      (e.g. https://192.168.4.2:8081)
  - It's a self-signed cert, so tap Advanced > Proceed / Continue.
  - Enter the PIN from the dashboard Calibrate tab (currently 2079).
  - Follow the on-screen CALIBRATION WALK: it guides you to spots/route around the
    room; walk it holding the phone. This teaches the system the room (takes a couple
    of minutes). Without it there is no position yet.

------------------------------------------------
  STEP 5 - TRACK + THE CROWD DEMO
------------------------------------------------
  - After calibration, your phone shows as a moving dot on the dashboard / 3D view.
  - Press the Uno Q WHITE BUTTON -> a crowd of simulated "phones" appears and
    SWARMS AROUND YOU. Walk around: you blend into the crowd and are hard to pick
    out. A yellow "SIMULATED crowd active" banner shows while it's on (it's honest -
    those decoys are simulated and nothing is transmitted).
  - Press the button again -> the crowd disappears, just you remain.

  To stop everything: click the terminal from Step 3 and press Ctrl+C.

------------------------------------------------
  IF THINGS AREN'T WORKING
------------------------------------------------
  - Board dots grey / map empty  -> you're not on 192.168.4.x, or firewall (Step 0),
                                     or a board isn't powered. See DEMO_STEPS.txt.
  - Phone page won't load         -> phone not on WATCHING-YOU, wrong 192.168.4.x,
                                     or you used http:// instead of https://.
  - No dot after calibrating      -> finish the whole walk; keep the phone ON the
                                     WATCHING-YOU Wi-Fi the entire time.
  - Button does nothing           -> Uno Q plugged in by USB? Only one button program
                                     at a time (demo_run --button already runs it).
