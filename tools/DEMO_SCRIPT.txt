================================================================
  MOTH - LIVE DEMO PRESENTATION SCRIPT
  Read this while you present. [SAY] = talking points, [DO] = actions.
  Target length: ~4-5 minutes. Keep it conversational, not word-for-word.
================================================================

WHO DOES WHAT (can be 1-3 people):
  - OPERATOR  : drives the laptop / dashboard, narrates.
  - WALKER    : holds the phone, walks around the room.
  - BUTTON    : presses the Uno Q white button on cue (can be the Walker).

BEFORE THE AUDIENCE ARRIVES (see tools/REAL_DEMO.txt for detail):
  [ ] Boards powered, laptop on WATCHING-YOU (ipconfig shows 192.168.4.x)
  [ ] Server running:  py tools/demo_run.py --button   -> dashboard http://127.0.0.1:8080
  [ ] 3 board dots are GREEN, boards dragged to their real spots (Setup/Layout)
  [ ] Phone joined WATCHING-YOU, calibration walk DONE, your dot shows and moves
  [ ] Projector/screen mirrors the dashboard
  Have a backup plan ready (see "IF IT BREAKS" at the bottom).

----------------------------------------------------------------
  SCENE 1 - THE HOOK  (~30s)
----------------------------------------------------------------
[SAY] "This is Moth. It finds where people are in a room using nothing but
       Wi-Fi signal strength - no app, no GPS, no camera. Just a few $10 boards
       and a laptop."
[SAY] "It never reads any content - only the radio header: who sent a frame, on
       what network, and how strong it was. That alone is enough to place you."
[DO]  Show the dashboard / 3D room with the 3 board dots.
[SAY] "These three dots are our Wi-Fi boards, mounted around the room. They're
       the sensors - they don't move."

----------------------------------------------------------------
  SCENE 2 - LIVE TRACKING  (~60s)   <- the "wow"
----------------------------------------------------------------
[DO]  WALKER starts walking a slow loop, holding the phone.
[SAY] "My phone joined the room's Wi-Fi. Watch the dot - that's me, being tracked
       in real time by signal strength alone."
[DO]  Point at the moving dot; let it track the walker for a few seconds.
[SAY] "No app is reporting my location. The room is figuring it out from the
       outside. This is what Wi-Fi can reveal about you without your help."
  (If accuracy is rough, own it:)
[SAY] "It's a quick calibration, so it's a bit jumpy - with a longer setup it
       gets to about half a metre."

----------------------------------------------------------------
  SCENE 3 - THE PROBLEM  (~20s)
----------------------------------------------------------------
[SAY] "Here's the uncomfortable part: you didn't have to install anything to be
       found. So how do you hide in a room that's watching the Wi-Fi?"
[SAY] "Our answer: don't jam it, don't hack it - get lost in a crowd."

----------------------------------------------------------------
  SCENE 4 - THE DEFENSE: THE CROWD  (~60s)   <- the payoff
----------------------------------------------------------------
[DO]  BUTTON presses the Uno Q WHITE BUTTON.
[SAY] "One button. Now the room sees a crowd of phones instead of just me."
[DO]  Point out the burst of extra dots around the walker. The yellow
      "SIMULATED crowd active" banner appears.
[SAY] "Be honest about this: that crowd is SIMULATED - decoys we draw on the
       screen. We are NOT transmitting fake signals into the air. It's a safe,
       display-only demonstration of a real idea: a room full of similar devices
       makes any one person hard to pick out."
[DO]  WALKER keeps moving - ideally weave among the decoy dots.
[SAY] "Which dot is me now? When I move with the crowd, my real position blends
       in and gets lost in the noise. That's the whole point - more addresses in
       the room means less certainty about any single person."
[DO]  BUTTON presses the white button again -> crowd disappears.
[SAY] "Button off - and I'm exposed again. On, I'm hidden. Off, I'm found."

----------------------------------------------------------------
  SCENE 5 - WHAT'S REAL vs WHAT'S SIMULATED  (~30s)  (keep it honest)
----------------------------------------------------------------
[SAY] "To be clear about what you just saw:"
[SAY] "  REAL:      the tracking. Those boards really located me from Wi-Fi."
[SAY] "  SIMULATED: the crowd. Those decoys are drawn in the view and labelled
                    as simulated; nothing was broadcast and the real measurement
                    was never touched."
[SAY] "We did it the honest way on purpose - it shows the privacy concept
       without building a tool that interferes with anyone else's network."

----------------------------------------------------------------
  SCENE 6 - CLOSE  (~20s)
----------------------------------------------------------------
[SAY] "So Moth shows two things: how much plain Wi-Fi can reveal about where you
       are, and one honest way to take some of that back - disappear into a crowd."
[SAY] "Hardware: three ESP32 boards and an Arduino for the button. Everything
       runs offline on this laptop. Happy to take questions."

LIKELY QUESTIONS (have an answer ready):
  Q: Can it read my messages?       A: No - only the radio header, never content.
  Q: Does the crowd really jam Wi-Fi? A: No - it's a simulated, display-only
                                         demo; nothing is transmitted.
  Q: Does it need my permission?     A: In connected mode you choose to join the
                                         network; leave it and you disappear.
  Q: How accurate?                   A: ~0.5 m with a full calibration; rougher
                                         in a quick demo setup.

----------------------------------------------------------------
  IF IT BREAKS (stay calm, keep talking)
----------------------------------------------------------------
  - Dot won't track / boards grey -> narrate from the 3D room + go straight to
    the crowd button (decoys still appear even without live tracking). See
    DEMO_STEPS.txt for the real fix (usually not on 192.168.4.x, or firewall).
  - Nothing works at all -> fall back to the simulator so the show still runs:
        py tools/demo_run.py --sim --button
    This gives a moving "SimPhone" + the crowd, no hardware needed. Just tell the
    audience it's running on simulated data.
  - Phone page won't load -> wrong IP or you used http:// (needs https://<laptop
    192.168.4.x>:8081) - skip it and demo with the dot already tracking.
