Where the boards are

Measure from one room corner (0,0, top-left on the map) to each board with a tape or a phone measuring app. Six inches either way is fine. Saving rebuilds the radio map.

  1. On a phone joined to the demo network, open the address above.
  2. Stand somewhere, tap that spot on the phone's map and press I'm here (hold still 8 s). Each mark first measures how far off the map was there, then teaches it that spot.
  3. Walk straight to the next amber ring and repeat until the room is covered. The walks in between teach the compass and stride. (A guided walk is under More.)
