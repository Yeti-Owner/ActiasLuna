Where the boards are

Measure from one room corner (0,0, top-left on the map) to each board with a tape or a phone measuring app. Six inches either way is fine. Saving rebuilds the radio map.

  1. On a phone joined to the demo network, open the address above and enter the PIN. Tap Turn on motion.
  2. 1 · Walk: follow the numbered dots (roughly two minutes). Doing it twice helps.
  3. 2 · Check: stand on each blue square for 8 seconds to measure the real error.

Which settings actually help?

Walk and do the checks on the phone while recording (on by default). This replays those recordings with each setting flipped and scores them against where you really were. About a minute.

Saved recordings

Recordings hold raw telemetry only (times, board, MAC, signal strength). Replays run the current layout and calibration over them. Delete them after a demo.
